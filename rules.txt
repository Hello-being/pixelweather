# Pixel Weather Station: Game Rules

## Goal

Keep the town's mood meter between 1 and 99 for the full 90 seconds.

## Mood Meter

- The mood meter starts at **50**.
- It ranges from **0 to 100**.
- If it reaches **0** or **100**, the game ends and you lose.

## Weather

The weather changes every **5 to 8 seconds**, chosen at random from:

| Weather | Effect on mood |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sunny | Slowly increases mood |
| Rain | Decreases mood unless the umbrella is up |
| Wind | Decreases mood unless the shelter covers the houses |
| Lightning | Sudden drop of 8 mood, with a white flash |

## Shelter and Umbrella

- The **shelter** moves left and right. It covers the houses when it sits between the left and right parts of the screen.
- The **umbrella** blocks rain. Press **SPACE** to raise it for **3 seconds**.
- After the umbrella goes down, it has a **5-second cooldown** before you can use it again.

## Winning and Losing

- **Win:** Survive 90 seconds with the mood never reaching 0 or 100.
- **Lose:** The mood reaches 0 or 100 at any time.

## Controls

- **Left / Right arrows or A / D:** Move the shelter
- **SPACE:** Raise the umbrella (or start and restart on the title and end screens)
- **R:** Restart the game
- **ESC:** Quit

## Scoring Notes

There is no point score. The game is won by lasting 90 seconds in balance.
